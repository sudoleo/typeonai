1. What consens.io is

consens.io sends your query to several third-party AI language models in parallel, shows their answers side by side, and can generate a consolidated consensus answer. The service is operated by the provider named in the Imprint (the "operator"). It is currently a free, non-commercial project. Features and usage limits are described in the app. AI results have the limitations explained below; this does not exclude mandatory statutory rights.

2. Processing by third-party AI providers

Your request is processed through OpenRouter and its inference/search endpoints. The available model families include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, xAI, DeepSeek, Moonshot AI/Kimi, Z.ai/GLM and Meta. A model's developer and its actual hosting provider can differ. Consensus, evaluation, context and Memory features may use additional configured models; Watches and API runs use a server-selected configuration. Deselecting one comparison model does not exclude all processing by that company or country.

Our inference calls request ZDR routing, but this does not cover web-search tools or every kind of operational metadata. Processing can occur outside the EU/EEA, including in the USA, Singapore or China, depending on the endpoint. See the Privacy Notice for data flows, saved conversations and international transfers.

7. Public sharing of consensus answers

Separately, operator-configured Scheduled Publisher and Topic runs can publish AI-generated material automatically. Private conversation storage is not public sharing.

  • A shared page contains the question, the consensus answer, the differences analysis, the list of sources, and the names of the AI models consulted as a snapshot at the time of sharing. It does not display your account profile or account ID. However, personal information that appears in your question or the generated content will also become public.
  • By creating a shared page you confirm that the question and any content you contributed to it do not infringe third-party rights (including copyright and privacy rights) and are not unlawful.

Search-engine indexing of shared pages is disabled by default and can be enabled for selected pages through operator-controlled publication and indexing settings, including the Scheduled Publisher.

Reports are stored only as an anonymous counter per reason (for example "inaccurate", "harmful", "spam"); we do not store the reporter's IP address, browser data, or any other personal data with a report. Repeated reports automatically remove a page from search-engine indexing until we have reviewed it.
