Independent AI tooling

About consens.io

consens.io is a small independent tool for comparing AI model answers, spotting agreement, and making disagreements easier to inspect.

Why not just ask one model?

Any single model can be confidently wrong, and one answer never tells you which case you're in. consens.io sends your prompt to several models at once, lets you inspect their individual responses, and adds a consensus layer that shows where they agree, and flags where they don't.

Does the consensus actually work?

We publish an evaluation on 314 closed-book MMLU-Pro questions, with the tested models, method and limitations on the benchmark page. This is a limited evaluation of a particular configuration, not proof that consensus is generally more accurate. Models can share the same error; agreement scores are not probabilities that an answer is correct.

See the benchmark

What it does

You enter a single prompt and receive multiple model responses next to each other. The consensus step then summarizes common ground, highlights contradictions, and gives you a compact answer as a starting point.

It is useful for research, learning, drafting, and everyday questions, but it is not a substitute for your own judgement.

How it works

  • You choose which models to query and adjust the available options.
  • The backend sends requests through OpenRouter to model endpoints and may use web-search services.
  • Responses are rendered side by side for direct comparison.
  • Additional AI calls produce a consensus and evaluate overlaps and differences.

Project and maintainer

consens.io is built and maintained by Max Plack in Germany as a privately operated, currently non-monetised project. Operator and contact details are in the Imprint. It started as a private workflow for sanity-checking AI answers and gradually turned into a public side project.

There is no large company behind it, no marketing team, and no dedicated support department. The project is iterated as time and budget allow.

What it costs

Nothing. It's free while I'm testing it. There are limits because a run can call up to six comparison models, plus synthesis and evaluation models, and costs me real money. That's the entire reason, and it's easier to say than to dress up as a plan.

The same goes for the features that are switched off: those are the expensive runs, so I enable them for individual accounts instead of for everyone. There's nothing to buy today either. But if people keep telling me they want those runs regularly, a membership may be an option in future. Any paid offer would require a separate express agreement; free use does not automatically become paid. You can send feedback. I read everything at contact@consens.io, feedback and complaints included.

If you need more than the limit allows in the meantime, enter your own OpenRouter API key in the app; requests still pass through our server and OpenRouter charges your account under its terms. Have fun with it :)

Data handling

  • Prompts, enabled Memory and conversation context are used to call AI models and, when invoked, search services.
  • Signed-in Consensus conversations and their sidebar bookmarks are saved automatically until you delete them or your account. Publishing a private conversation is a separate action.
  • Login and account features are handled through authentication infrastructure.
  • If you use your own API keys, they are stored locally in your browser and only pass through the server to reach the selected provider; they are never stored server-side.
  • There are no embedded ad-network tracking pixels. We use Umami Cloud for usage analytics, as described in the Privacy Notice.

For full details, read the Privacy Notice. Avoid secrets, highly sensitive data, and information that normally requires professional advice.
