Consensus Answer

The Nile is the conventional longest river, but the Amazon has a credible claim.

By convention and in most references the Nile leads at about 6,650 km Whether the Amazon overtakes it depends on where its mouth is drawn: counting the Pará estuary adds several hundred kilometres, and not every survey accepts that boundary.

  • The Amazon is unambiguously the largest river by discharge
  • The disputed 2007 expedition puts the Amazon at 6,992 km.

A careful answer names both rivers and the measurement dispute instead of hiding it inside one confident number.

What it costs

Free while I'm testing it.

consens.io is a one-person project and still very much a test. Every question you send calls six model providers at once, and that bill lands on me. That's the only reason there are limits at all.

Today

Nothing to buy right now

No checkout, no price, no waiting list. Where something is switched off, it's one of the expensive runs, and those I enable per account rather than for everyone, because they cost a multiple of a normal one.

The limits

The cap is what I can afford

Your remaining runs are shown in the app, and that number moves with what the month allows. You can also put your own provider API keys into the settings; then my limits stop applying and the costs are yours.

Later

If enough of you want it, it'll come

There's no membership today and I'm not collecting sign-ups for one. But if people keep telling me they want the expensive runs regularly, that's how I'd make it possible. Write to me either way at contact@consens.io, and have fun with it :)
